SCW - Scratch Console Wars

Yearly console competition hosted on scratch by @FroggyRaptor

The SCW is a yearly competition done every year on scratch. competitors are give around a year to make their console then release it to compete with other consoles. The tradition was started by @FroggyRaptor and @24AkuwZi and has since snowballed into a still small but bigger, yearly event with many scratchers from all over joining to see who can outwit the others
